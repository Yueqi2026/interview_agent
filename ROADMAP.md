# Roadmap after v0.4

## v0.5 — Persistent contributor loop
Implement production authentication, PostgreSQL schema/migrations, contributor profile persistence, interview-session persistence, structured extraction review and consent audit records.

## v0.6 — RAG foundation
Generate KnowledgeChunks only from approved material, create embeddings, add pgvector retrieval, source-aware Agent responses and deletion/re-index jobs.

## v0.7 — Live intelligence
Add dated company/JD/public-source retrieval, freshness rules, citations and strict separation between personal experience and current information.

## v0.8 — Mock interview
Role-specific interview plans, adaptive follow-ups, transcript, rubric-based feedback and a post-interview review report.

## v0.9 — Safety/admin beta
PII and confidential-information detection, moderation queue, reports, consent audit UI, deletion/export tools, rate limits and observability.

## v1.0 — Closed beta
Seed 30–50 high-quality Agents, recruit 100–300 job seekers, measure search→chat conversion, average conversation depth, repeat usage and “better than generic AI” perceived value.
